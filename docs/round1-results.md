The historical Q1 logistic macro F1 of 0.225, majority score of 0.207 and two-voice recall 3/24 use the previous grouped allocation. The new, more balanced allocation puts all three classes in every test fold. Differences across allocations do not estimate a model gain. The paired forest comparison fixes the allocation.

Clean Q4 point indices rank modulated > two-voice > flat at grid points 20–50.5 m, and two-voice > modulated > flat at 51–150 m. At 100 m the indices are 0.244, 0.369 and 0.448. The paired two-voice minus modulated interval includes zero at each of the four preselected comparison distances. Under 20% loss, flat fails both cutoffs at 100 m and two-voice meets both at 100 m, under either response link in the point estimates. Modulated meets the 100 m cutoff only under the common threshold and acoustic-only loss, with 55% of conditional draws meeting that cutoff. At 150 m two-voice meets both cutoffs under acoustic-only loss and falls below both under coupled loss. These are scenario results. They support using multiple disturbance assumptions when prioritizing monitoring locations. A site-specific vessel buffer requires measured noise and behavioral data.
