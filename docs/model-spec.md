- Q2 clips the equal-weight mean of three available-case marginal correlations to [0, 1], fits isotonic knot values, then interpolates in log distance. Contrast weight sets and unconstrained log interpolation. Bootstrap wide rows independently within each metric file, preserving distances in a row. Use aligned rows as a dependence sensitivity where missingness patterns are identical, without assuming identity of experimental units. Intervals are conditional on these sampling models. Thresholds 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, and 0.7. Report interval bounds and censoring.
- Q3 compares paired any-response rates and uses regularized multinomial logistic regression for behavior masks 0 through 7. Inputs are assigned natural call type, stimulus type, their interaction, context, log distance, and log group size. Report any-response as one minus probability of mask 0. Estimate log latency among observed responders using regularized regression and report conditional medians and event counts. Use actual latency columns, and describe counts/durations separately. Keep pair groups intact in CV and bootstrap. Flag local sparsity and unavailable conditional latencies.
- Q4 standardizes over all experiment contexts at eight birds on the common 20–150 m range. Use fidelity times natural-stimulus any-response probability with a per-call fixed clean 50% reference at 20 m. Use a sustained downward crossing and explicit censoring. Contrast behavior-only and acoustic-only, sensitivity thresholds and weights, and report fixed-reference environmental scenarios. Threshold scenarios gate the index when fidelity falls below 0.6 or 0.7. Multiply fidelity by retained fractions for 5%–20% loss. Treat probability changes under noise as a modeled scenario using attenuation and a control response floor, rather than an experimentally estimated effect. Median group size and a range of assumed fractions convert group probabilities into expected responding individuals under scenario assumptions.
