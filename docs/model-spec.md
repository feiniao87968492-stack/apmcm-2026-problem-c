Use F02 availability as a diagnostic and fold-median pulse imputation as a sensitivity. | F02 is present only for two-voice calls. Average signal quality is expected to decline with distance, although individual readings fluctuate. Equal weighting avoids estimating perceptual importance from an unrelated behavioral experiment. |
| Q2 uncertainty | Use fixed thresholds 0.40, 0.50, 0.60 for potential, standard and reliable recognition and 0.70 as a stricter scenario. Independently resample complete wide rows by representation. Repeat with aligned-row draws when missingness masks match. At support limits report bounds. | Thresholds turn signal similarity into an operational range. Distance observations stay together within each sampled row. The attachments lack acquisition identifiers, so both interval conventions are conditional on their row-resampling assumptions. |
| Q3 behavior | Encode head, vocal and approach with bits 1, 2 and 4. Compare paired any-response rates descriptively. Fit a standardized L2 multinomial model (C=1) to mask using assigned call, stimulus, interaction, context, log(1+distance) and log(1+group size). Validate in five expID-grouped folds against pooled training frequencies and resample pairs intact. | The model accounts for co-occurring behaviors using one probability distribution. | Q3 latency | For each behavior fit ridge regression (alpha=5) to log(1+seconds) among observed responders, with a Gaussian residual model on that scale. Report response probability, conditional median and sample support at 50, 100 and 150 m and eight birds. Average contexts using empirical frequencies and weight the conditional latency mixture by event probability. A stratum with no observed latency has no estimated median. Flag fewer than five observed latencies within 25 m as sparse. | The transformation keeps observed zero onsets. The two outcomes address different questions about behavior. The support flag gives the reader local sample context. |
| Q4 integration | Combine fidelity and standardized natural-response probability by multiplication. Use the common 20–150 m domain, a 0.5 m grid, median group size eight and empirical context weights. Use the final above-cutoff location to interpolate the sustained crossing at 50% of the call's clean index at 20 m. Keep that reference fixed across scenarios. Check 40% and 60%, component-only models and Q2 weight choices. | A call should retain signal and elicit behavior to score strongly. | Q4 scenarios | Gate the index below fidelity thresholds 0.50, 0.60 and 0.70. Evaluate uniform losses of 5%, 10%, 15% and 20%. Apply the resulting retention to the positive natural-response excess over its matched control prediction. For expected counts assume 25%, 50% or 75% of eight birds respond when any response occurs. Pair Q2 and Q3 draws by index from separately seeded analyses. | These transparent perturbations isolate how the operational result depends on recognition, retention and the group-to-individual assumption. The two independent sources of data give a conditional combined uncertainty assessment. |
