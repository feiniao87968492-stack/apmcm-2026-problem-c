# Independent verification of cost-sensitive monitoring

**Pass.** From raw Data01, I independently reconstructed the 16 date-by-location groups and five frozen validation folds. I standardized the six features within each training fold using its mean and population standard deviation. The independently fitted multinomial model reproduces the archived probabilities to within 1e-13 and matches the archived hard labels on all 108 recordings. I also reconciled the archived hard XGBoost labels against the original boosting export. All probability models used the full training complements, and evaluation used the 105 FalseBay holdouts from 15 dates. [Frozen specification](../docs/model-spec.md#q1-to-q4-cost-sensitive-monitoring-freeze) · [Archived predictions](../results/q1_class_prior_predictions.csv) · [Boosting predictions](../results/round2_q1_predictions.csv)

I checked the passing sets directly against the published gross curves and their fixed cutoff of 0.2875981988873035. I independently fitted one binary logistic model per passing set and one proportional-odds model per fold. Each ordinal fit converged, and I converted its linear predictor and fitted thresholds to cumulative probabilities with the logistic function. The multinomial and ordinal probabilities are nested on every scored row, with the two-voice probability bounded above by the modulated-or-two-voice probability. [Gross curves](../results/q4_comparison_curves.csv) · [Holdout predictions](../results/q1_q4_cost_predictions.csv)

I derived the decision threshold from the expected conditional costs. A pass costs a times one minus the estimated probability, and a nonpass costs b times that probability, for false-pass cost a and missed-pass cost b. The inclusive threshold is a/(a+b), giving 0.50, 0.25 and 0.75 for the three cost ratios. I independently calculated Brier score and clipped log loss. For paired intervals I multiplied per-date cost totals and recording counts by each bootstrap draw's date multiplicities, then divided the sampled cost difference by the sampled recording count. Against computation commit 715934a, all 105 prediction rows, 30 decision rows, 36 paired cost contrasts and six probability score rows agree. The largest probability discrepancy is 2.3e-16, decisions and error counts agree exactly, and cost estimates and interval endpoints agree within 1e-14. All 300 date-resampled replicates entered each interval. All six equal-cost primary contrasts have positive upper 95% endpoints. The frozen improvement criterion is therefore unmet. At a 3:1 false-pass to missed-pass cost ratio, all three probability rules reduce mean cost against both archived rules by reducing false passes, with all 21 passing recordings missed. For the wider passing set and a 1:3 cost ratio, all three rules reduce mean cost against both archived rules, with 43 false passes out of 44 nonpassing recordings. These tradeoffs are conditional on the modeled passing sets and the assumed costs. The primary probability scores favor the summed multinomial model on both metrics. For the wider passing set, proportional odds has the lowest Brier score and direct binary logistic has the lowest log loss.
