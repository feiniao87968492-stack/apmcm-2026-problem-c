# Independent verification of Q4 bounded-context behavioral noise sensitivity

**Pass.** I regenerated the two playback fits and the four acoustic curves from the original 4,800 paired draws and archive seeds 20261130 and 20261201. I reconstructed each draw's 20 m full-fit cutoff from the clean isotonic fidelity and the context-standardized signed response. At each distance I calculated the +6 dB acoustic retention and applied its power \(\lambda\) to positive response excess. I then multiplied the resulting signed excess by each curve's retained acoustic fidelity and tested all four curves at every half-metre node through 100 m. The clean indicators and the \(\lambda=1\) noisy indicators matched the six archived context columns for all 4,800 rows in each fit. The 12 archived vertex means and both fits' bounded extrema also agreed.
