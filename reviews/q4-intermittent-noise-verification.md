# Independent verification of Q4 intermittent noise at 100 m

**Pass.** I regenerated the archived acoustic and date-block draws and reconstructed the signed two-voice scores on all four shapes at the 161 nodes from 20 to 100 m. The cutoff for every draw is half the equal-call mean of the full-data clean isotonic score at 20 m. I used the same cutoff for both fits. The independent calculation solves node constraints analytically and then verifies the production bisection brackets. [Frozen specification](../docs/model-spec.md#q4-intermittent-noise-at-100-m-freeze)

For a positive clean score $S$, let $T$ denote its draw's cutoff and let $t=(10^{0.6}-1)u/(1+u)$, where $u=(d/D)^g$ comes from the shared two-voice acoustic fit. The +6 dB score is $S_6=S/(1+t)$. The largest feasible $f$ at that node is $(S-T)/(S-S_6)$ for the mean score and $(S/T-1)/t$ for the continuous mean-power score. A node with nonpositive clean score always fails because the signed score remains nonpositive and every cutoff is positive. The minimum across the four shapes and all nodes gives each draw's limit. The 3,840th largest limit across draws yields the criterion boundary. For the state-indicator rule the exact crossing is $(C_0-3840)/(C_0-C_6)$, where $C_0$ and $C_6$ are the clean and +6 dB prefix counts. [Production implementation](../q4_intermittent_noise.py)
