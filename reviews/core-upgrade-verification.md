# Independent numerical review of the core model upgrade

**Pass for the reported comparisons and production decisions.** I scored the exported holdouts independently and reproduced the empirical Q3 baselines, the isotonic Q2 comparator and the integrated Q4 scenarios from their inputs. The checks below distinguish statistics recalculated from exported predictions from models independently fitted to raw data. [Frozen specification](../docs/model-spec.md)

## Q1 classification

I reconstructed the recording group identities and all five training prevalence vectors from the original records and held-group IDs. From the six held-out probability arrays I recomputed pooled macro F1, all class recalls, log loss and summed Brier. I independently replayed the 1,000 recording-group resamples and checked each macro F1 gain interval. The physical-feature forest gives 0.39156 macro F1 versus 0.31441 for the fixed logistic model. Its paired gain interval is [0.02216, 0.15071]. It improves log loss and Brier over the prevalence predictor, while its two-voice recall is 0/24 against 2/24 for logistic. The frozen joint criterion therefore retains fixed logistic. [Held-out probabilities](../results/core_q1_oof.csv) · [Scores](../results/core_q1_scores.csv)

## Q2 propagation

I read the nine raw propagation CSVs, computed the available-case equal-weight correlations, and matched each held-out observed value. I refitted the isotonic comparator independently in all eight holdouts and confirmed log-distance interpolation and last-knot forward prediction. I recalculated every aggregate point signed error and RMSE from the prediction cells. I applied the frozen comparison and tie-breaking rule to the published paired intervals. The selected 0.0025 log-exponent penalty gives interior / forward RMSE 0.04547 / 0.10703 versus 0.06816 / 0.24843 for isotonic. Its paired RMSE differences have intervals [−0.02368, −0.02179] and [−0.14681, −0.13674]. The fixed absorption candidate passes the replacement gate and improves on its matched no-absorption ablation in forward prediction by −0.00503 [−0.00528, −0.00471]. Its interior RMSE of 0.04899 places it behind the chosen curve under the frozen interior-first rule. Conditional paired intervals were taken from the archived results for this selection check. ## Q3 playback masks and timing

From raw experiment IDs, dates, natural tracks and distances I reconstructed each ordinary and tail training and test complement. The ordinary folds cover 139 intact pairs. Tail validation covers 26 pairs, with 33 arm records at 125 m or farther. I independently replayed the half-count mask and six-cell timing fits and checked all pooled empirical-baseline scores against the released tables. I recomputed ordinary mask comparison intervals from the date-level scores using the test trial counts and frozen 10,000 date resamples. The rank-one tilt's point log loss and Brier improve on both references, but its mask log-loss interval versus stimulus frequencies spans zero, as do its Brier intervals versus both references. The remaining candidates also miss the joint criterion. The full multinomial remains the distance-specific mask production model. ## Q4 component alignment and planning scenarios

I independently joined selected acoustic and Q3 predictions by call and distance. The clean gross reference is 0.2875982. I recalculated acoustic loss, coupled excess loss, gross scores, running conjunctions and the linear gross threshold crossings. Every dense row and all reported gross and conjunctive radii agree, including range flags. The unsupported arm-distance combinations are identifiable from the recorded natural and control ranges and local support. Archived versus selected mask substitutions coincide because the adoption decision retained the full multinomial. The conjunction instead uses its separately selected timing model. For the selected propagation and 20% coupled loss, gross radii are 46.34, 81.65 and 117.38 m in flat/modulated/two-voice order. The 30 s conjunctive radii are 58.0, 107.5 and 132.0 m, identical to the 15 s radii under the selected timing model.
