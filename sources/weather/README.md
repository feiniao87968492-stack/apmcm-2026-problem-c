# Hourly weather used for acoustic propagation

The archived CSV contains the joint weather rows used by the Monte Carlo calculations. Temperature and relative humidity are at 2 m. Pressure is surface pressure in hPa. Wind is the ERA5 10 m speed in m/s. Times are local in Africa/Johannesburg (UTC+02). The underlying ERA5 data are supplied by the Copernicus Climate Change Service and were obtained through Open-Meteo on 2026-11-28. [ERA5 single levels](https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.adbb2d47) · [Historical Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api)

- **False Bay.** One request per year for 2013–2022, June 1 through July 31. The requested point is 34.20° S, 18.62° E with sea-cell selection. The returned grid point is 34.25° S, 18.50° E at sea level. Six daytime hours per day from 08:00 through 13:00 contribute 3,660 weather vectors. [Example 2022 request](https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive?latitude=-34.20&longitude=18.62&start_date=2022-06-01&end_date=2022-07-31&hourly=temperature_2m,relative_humidity_2m,surface_pressure,wind_speed_10m&timezone=Africa%2FJohannesburg&models=era5&cell_selection=sea&wind_speed_unit=ms)
- **Brandvlei Dam vicinity.** December 2021 month at 33.73° S, 19.40° E with nearest-cell selection. The response identifies 33.75° S, 19.50° E at 509 m elevation. The same six daytime hours and a wind screen of at most 3 m/s yield 142 weather vectors. The exact propagation trial time is unavailable. These vectors represent possible conditions near the dam. [December 2021 request](https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive?latitude=-33.73&longitude=19.40&start_date=2021-12-01&end_date=2021-12-31&hourly=temperature_2m,relative_humidity_2m,surface_pressure,wind_speed_10m&timezone=Africa%2FJohannesburg&models=era5&cell_selection=nearest&wind_speed_unit=ms)
